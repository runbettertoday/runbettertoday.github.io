About Run Better Today

Who this site is for

Run Better Today is for adult recreational runners who have mild hip, knee or ankle discomfort and want simple, research-informed exercises and a short daily routine. It is not for injuries that need urgent care, and it is not a replacement for seeing a qualified healthcare professional.

Who makes it

The creator of this site is not a doctor, physical therapist or licensed healthcare professional. The content has not been reviewed by a clinician. It summarises published guidelines and research in plain language for educational purposes.

How the content is sourced

Each exercise and recommendation links to the guideline, review or study it is based on. Every source is listed with its publication details and links on the Sources page. We do not invent citations, and a listed source does not imply endorsement by its authors or publisher.

Each item carries an evidence label:

  • Strong: supported by a clinical guideline, consensus statement, or meta-analysis of trials.
  • Practice: common clinical practice, expert opinion in guidelines, or a single trial.
  • Low: low-certainty or mixed evidence.

Exercises may help reduce discomfort for some people. Results vary, and nothing on this site is a promise of any outcome.

Medical disclaimer

Run Better Today provides general educational information only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional. Using this site does not create a doctor-patient, therapist-patient or any other professional relationship. Do not ignore professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you read here.

Stop exercising and get checked if you notice any warning signs. Get clearance from a healthcare professional before starting if you are under 18, pregnant, have a health condition, or are recovering from surgery.

In an emergency, call 911 (US) or your local emergency number.
