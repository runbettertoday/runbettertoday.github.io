Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

No accounts

This site has no accounts or sign-ups, and it doesn't ask for your name or email.

What is stored, and where

The weekly load tracker, training notes, and feedback form save what you enter only in your own browser, using local storage. That information stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved feedback with the "Delete all" button on the feedback page, delete tracker weeks individually, clear the notes box, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

No selling or sharing

We don't sell or share your data. We don't receive it in the first place.

Changes

If we ever add a server, a way to send feedback to us, or analytics, we will update this policy before doing so, and change the effective date below.

Questions? Email runbetter@mail.grokbot.com.
